# Showreel plan (cut 2)

**Angle:** "In production, not in a demo." Show the real products, live, not the portfolio's own components.
**Look:** deliberately unlike the site: warm light stage, each company in its own brand tint, Inter + Instrument Serif,
real browser and phone frames scrolling through each live site, horizontal push transitions.
**Format:** 1920x1080, 30 fps, 24 s, 120 BPM (every cut on a beat). Music: original, synthesized, F major.

| Time | Scene | On screen |
|---|---|---|
| 0-3 | Hook | "In production, not in a demo." Four live product windows fan in, one per beat. |
| 3-6.5 | Grupp | trygrupp.africa desktop + mobile scrolling to "Unlock Custom-Built Tools". Software Engineer, banking + BaaS. |
| 6.5-10 | WorkSage | worksage.ai scrolling to "Your work is everywhere". Forward-Deployed Engineer via Gearsparks. |
| 10-13.5 | Throughline | throughline.team scrolling to the plan dashboard. Fullstack Engineer, V2 backend. |
| 13.5-17 | ShipPack | theshippack.com scrolling to "Built for flexibility and control". Software Developer, tracking + pricing. |
| 17-21 | Own builds | Reelstack (dashboard, search, landing), QRaven and Zush phones, screens swapping on the beat. |
| 21-24 | Outro | "Jay. Software & applied AI engineer." jayfolio.dev, over a drifting mosaic of every product. |

**Sources:** live public marketing sites (captured as a visitor) and screenshots committed in the Reelstack,
QRaven-mobile and Zush repos. No private source code, internal dashboards, or personal data (Reelstack leaderboard
and the QRaven profile screen were left out).
