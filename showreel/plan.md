# Brag plan: Jay / studio showreel

## Inspect answers
- **What is it:** Jay, an independent engineer working like a one-person studio, shipping web apps, mobile apps and AI agents end to end.
- **Who for / what it does for them:** teams that need software in production, not a demo: contracts and full-time hiring teams.
- **What sets it apart:** whole-stack ownership plus agents that are instrumented ("Not a demo. A trace you can read.").
- **Most impressive claim:** shipped with 6 product teams (Grupp, Sovalabs, Throughline, Gearsparks, Cleverfolks, Shippack) and a deep bench of Go systems (Warden, Reverie, Specter, Sanctum, PrivateTM, Qraven).
- **Visual hook:** the site's own hero type: ultra-condensed Hubot Sans rising out of a mask, volt on ink.
- **Real UI to show:** the site's real components and CSS: project cards (browser / phone / terminal frames), the agent trace, the project index with its width-axis stretch, the client marquee, the volt contact block.
- **Tone:** polished with cinematic type: serious, elegant, confident. Not a joke project.
- **Share caption:** see share-copy.txt.

## Angle
"One person. Every layer." The width axis of the display type is the signature motion: names stretch from 75% to 125% as they land.

## Data hygiene
No placeholder metrics ([N], [X]%) appear anywhere. The trace scene shows step names and details only, no invented timings or token counts. No personal data, no email or phone number.

## Format
Landscape 1920x1080, 30fps, 21s. Music at 120 BPM (beat = 0.5s = 15 frames, bar = 2s), every cut on a beat.
Key content kept in the centre so the hero's 16:8 (desktop) and 4:5 (phone, object-fit: cover) crops still read.

## Sound
Original track synthesized for this video, 120 BPM, A minor (Am - F - C - G, one bar each).
- 0-4s: filtered pad + soft kick, noise riser into 4s.
- 4-14s: full groove: kick, clap on 2 and 4, offbeat hats, sidechained sub bass, pluck arpeggio.
- 12-14s: lift: riser into the company slam.
- 14-17s: full + accent hits on each name.
- 17s: chord stab on the volt wipe, groove thins out, ring-out to 21s.
SFX: soft low-passed whooshes on cuts and in-key ticks on card lands, mixed under the music on the same grid.
